Helpful Tips Before You Call a Technician

5 Signs Your Refrigerator Needs Professional Repair

A warm fridge, pooling water, or a loud compressor noise can all signal trouble. As a trusted provider of Refrigerator Repair, we see these warning signs every week across North Hollywood kitchens.

Ignoring small issues — like a door seal that doesn't close tight or frost buildup in the freezer — often leads to bigger, more expensive repairs down the line. Catching the problem early saves both your food and your budget.

Our certified technicians diagnose the root cause the same day, whether it's a faulty thermostat, a failing compressor, or a simple clogged drain line.

If your refrigerator isn't keeping food cold, call 800-860-0974 for fast, affordable service.

Why Choose a Certified Technician for High-End Appliances

Luxury brands like Sub Zero, Wolf, Viking and Thermador use specialized components that general handymen often aren't trained to repair correctly.

A Certified Appliance Technician understands the precise calibration these premium appliances need, protecting your investment and avoiding repeat repairs.

Washer & Dryer Not Working? Here's What to Check First

Before scheduling a Washer Repair or Dryer Repair, a few quick checks can save you time: make sure the unit is plugged in, the breaker hasn't tripped, and lint filters are clear.

If your washer won't drain or your dryer runs but doesn't heat, these are common issues our technicians resolve quickly — often in a single visit.

Persistent issues usually point to a worn belt, faulty heating element, or pump failure, all of which require a trained eye to diagnose safely.
